How Anime TV Works

A multi-tier architecture built with privacy, resilience, and speed in mind.

01 of 03

Distributed Multi-Source Scraping

When you select an anime, our local decentralized resolver queries redundant stream nodes simultaneously and picks the highest bitrate, zero-buffer source in milliseconds.

  • Instant Stream Failover
  • No Central Single Point of Failure
  • Encrypted Stream Resolvers
02 of 03

Hardware-Accelerated Engine

Custom decoder pipelines offload AV1, HEVC, and VP9 video rendering directly to your GPU, cutting battery consumption by up to 55% on mobile and TV sticks.

  • ExoPlayer Custom Fork
  • Zero Frame Drops at 4K 60fps
  • Instant Seek & Buffer Ahead
03 of 03

Private Cloud Sync & Tracking

Two-way synchronization with AniList, MyAnimeList, and Kitsu. Your watch progress updates automatically in real time with zero personal telemetry stored on remote servers.

Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about safety, updates, and open-source contributions.

Is Anime TV really 100% free?

Yes. Anime TV is a passion project built under the MIT open-source license. There are zero subscription tiers, locked qualities, paywalls, or donation nag screens.

How does it block ads so effectively?

Anime TV resolves direct HLS video stream chunks at the network protocol layer without loading web view wrappers or host page JavaScript ad scripts. Ad trackers never execute.

Is Anime TV legal and safe to use?

Anime TV is an open-source media indexer and player tool. It does not host video files on proprietary servers. The entire client source code is publicly auditable on GitHub with zero telemetry or malware.

Can I sync my watch history with AniList / MyAnimeList?

Yes. Go to Settings → Tracking in the app to connect your AniList or MyAnimeList account. Episodes will mark as watched automatically once you reach 80% completion.

How do I update the application?

Anime TV includes a built-in silent updater. When a new release is verified on GitHub, you will receive a single clean prompt on app start to update in 1-click.
