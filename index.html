Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with Anime TV.

Is Anime TV free?

Yes, Anime TV is 100% free and open source with no paywalls, hidden subscriptions, or locked features.

Is Anime TV legal and open source?

Anime TV is an open-source media scraper application built with community contributions and does not host content directly on proprietary servers.

Which platforms are supported?

Android smartphones, Android TV boxes, Amazon Fire TV devices, and Windows 10/11 are actively supported.

Where can I get the latest updates?

Check the Platforms section for direct installers, or visit our GitHub repository and Discord community for release notes and beta builds.
